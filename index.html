Research Interests

Causal Mediation & Heterogeneous Effects

M-learner frameworks for heterogeneous mediation analysis, high-dimensional omics mediators, and interpretable subgroup discovery in survival and clinical trial data.

Survival Analysis & Subgroup Identification

Dense random survival forests and related methods for identifying interpretable patient profiles with heterogeneous treatment benefit.

Surrogate Endpoints & Clinical Trials

Evaluation of heterogeneous surrogate endpoints and development of AI-assisted platforms for clinical trial analysis.

Digital Pathology & Statistical ML

Deep learning and statistical methods for biomarker prediction, survival outcomes, and treatment benefit from histopathology images.
