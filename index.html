Research Interests

Survival Analysis & Subgroup Identification

Methods for heterogeneous treatment effects in time-to-event data, including dense random survival forests and interpretable patient profiling for clinical decision making.

Causal Inference & Mediation

M-learner frameworks for heterogeneous mediation analysis, high-dimensional omics mediators, and surrogate endpoint evaluation in clinical trials.

Digital Pathology & Medical Imaging

Deep learning approaches for predicting biomarkers, survival outcomes, and treatment benefit from histopathology images, with emphasis on robustness and efficiency.

Tools for Medical Research

Development of practical software and LLM-assisted platforms for reconstructing Kaplan–Meier plots, calibrating survival models, and evaluating surrogate biomarkers.
